Introduction

Uy-Laurio Legal & Notarial Services ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Uy-Laurio client portal at uylaurio.vercel.app (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the Service. We are committed to handling your data responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Information We Collect

  • Account information. Your name, email address, and phone number when you register or sign in.
  • Google account data. If you sign in with Google, we receive your basic profile information (name and email address) as authorized by you through Google's consent screen.
  • Documents and case data. Files, requirements, and information you upload or submit in connection with your legal matter.
  • Usage data. Basic technical information such as log activity needed to keep the Service secure and reliable.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate your sign-in.
  • To provide, process, and track your legal and notarial service requests.
  • To communicate with you about your case status, requirements, and appointments.
  • To secure the Service and comply with our legal and professional obligations.

How We Store and Protect Your Data

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, our managed backend and authentication provider. Access is restricted through row-level security policies so that you can only view your own records. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with trusted service providers that help us operate the Service (such as our hosting and authentication providers), and with authorized personnel of the firm handling your matter. We may disclose information where required by law or legal process.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service and to comply with legal, regulatory, and professional record-keeping requirements.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
